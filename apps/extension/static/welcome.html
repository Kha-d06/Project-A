Qalaa Protect

Protection is on

Qalaa now checks the sites you open against its threat database and warns you before a known dangerous page loads. It is free, and it works without an account.

What the permissions are for

Read your browsing history (webNavigation, tabs)
To see which address each tab is opening so it can be checked. The check runs inside your browser: addresses aren't sent to the Qalaa server. Only short hash prefixes are, and only when a page might be on the list of known threats.
Manage your downloads
To check where a download comes from and pause it if the source is known to be dangerous.
Display notifications
To tell you about a paused download or the result of a link you asked it to check.
Block content (declarativeNetRequest) and alarms
To stop domains on Qalaa's list of known dangerous sites before they load. The whole list is downloaded every few hours, so nothing about your browsing is sent to get it.
Context menus and storage
For “Scan link with Qalaa”, and to remember your settings in this browser.

It never reads passwords, form contents, card details, messages or the content of pages. Link checks on hover and the on-screen assistant are off unless you turn them on; the assistant then asks for permission to run on pages and to read cookie names (never their values).

What it cannot do

It recognises sites that are already known to be malicious, plus strong warning signs such as look-alike domains. A brand-new scam site can pass these checks, and a page may begin loading before the warning appears. It does not replace the security software on your device.
